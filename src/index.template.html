Keyboard

  • U D L R F B turn a face. Hold Shift to go anticlockwise.
  • M E S turn the middle slices (3×3 and 5×5).
  • ← → ↑ ↓ turn the whole cube.
  • Space or H hint. N scramble. Z undo. Shift+Z redo.
  • G menu. P moves pad. Esc closes things.
